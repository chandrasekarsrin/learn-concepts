# Leadership styles
(from book: one minute manager)
- s1 directing (do it my way)
  - time constraint
  - junior developers
  - there is only one way to do it.

- s2 guiding (help you to do it in my way) 
  - who are interested
  - people who have potential and needs some guidance.
  - less time constraint
  - provide the benefit of my skills and experience by providing advice.

- s3 supporting (coaching style. I wll help you do it in your way)
  - not concerned about how you do something.
  - i am not expert but leading a competent team.
  - ask great questions. 

- s4 delegating (do it your way)
  - when leading competent and experts.
